% S5 Baseline Details
The four external baselines are re-implemented as planning policies within the same pipeline: the same robot,
executor and action checks, variants, seeds, planner model and evaluator, with only the planning and replanning
logic replaced. None of them uses our memory, \textit{IF} trigger, concurrent execution or corrective insertion.
Each receives the visible objects with their regions, the lid and gripper states and the camera image; an
object enters its state only once observed and remains known afterwards. The original methods were evaluated
with GPT-class models; here all use the selected planner. Planning-call budgets follow each method's own
replanning structure, so planner calls per trial are reported rather than equalized.

The distinctions that matter when reading the comparison are the following. \emph{VLM-TAMP} refines each
subgoal before executing it and re-prompts, with its history, only after a subgoal fails, at most twice; objects
revealed later therefore enter only through such a failure. \emph{OWL-TAMP} plans once from the initial scene
and executes the plan without re-querying the model, so objects hidden at planning time never enter its plan.
\emph{Inner Monologue} queries the model after every executed step with success and scene feedback, which makes
it the most expensive baseline in calls and time; its running dialogue is embedded in our zero-shot prompt
structure in place of the original few-shot task prompts. \emph{EPoG} plans from a goal scene graph built from
the goal specification for observed objects, without the original method's estimation of objects that have not
been seen, and calls the model only to resolve simulated motion errors. Its goal graph encodes final
object--region relations only, so it cannot represent the cooking procedure; its grill results reflect this
representation rather than a planning failure.

\noindent\textbf{ICL rows.} The baselines received Examples~1 and~2 of Section~\ref{sec:supp-icl} (the laundry
examples) in their own input and answer format, in grill requests only: as a full plan or a replan after a new
observation for VLM-TAMP, OWL-TAMP and Inner Monologue, and as error-resolution examples in EPoG's resolve
request. Because EPoG's model is used only for error resolution and its goal graph has no cooking procedure,
the examples cannot change its grill behavior. Kitchen prompts are unchanged, so each baseline's kitchen results
are shared between its zero-shot and ICL rows, as for our method.
