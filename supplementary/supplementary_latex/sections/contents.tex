% S1 Overview
This supplement provides technical detail that complements the main paper. Section~\ref{sec:supp-tasks}
gives the goal instructions, the evaluation conditions, the hidden contents of each variant and the trial
randomization. Section~\ref{sec:supp-impl} describes perception and memory, the \textit{IF},
\textit{WHEN} and \textit{WHERE} decisions, validation, recovery and termination, and the simulation
controllers. Section~\ref{sec:supp-prompts} shows the planner prompts, the in-context examples and a
recorded discovery correction with validation feedback. Section~\ref{sec:supp-baselines} lists the
adaptations of the external baselines that affect the comparison, and Section~\ref{sec:supp-config} the
inference settings and trial protocol. Notation follows the main paper.
